# Release Notes - v1.4 (Reports & Reminders)

**Date:** April 23, 2026
**Status:** Phase 10 closed with accepted scope adjustments

## Shipped
- **Reminder scheduling**: Local notifications for due dates, end-of-month missing payment reminders, and milestone notifications.
- **Reminder controls**: Settings support payment reminders, `1/3/7` day lead time, monthly reminders, and milestone reminders.
- **Permission UX**: Notification permission is requested on the first entry to Home after onboarding, with Settings retained as a fallback control surface.
- **Reports preview**: Preview UI is available from Settings before export.
- **PDF export**: Monthly, yearly, and full-history report ranges are supported, including amortization table export.
- **Share flow**: Reports and export artifacts can be shared through the system share sheet.
- **Stability**: Reminder/report routes, notification service behavior in tests, and related smoke coverage were hardened during closeout.

## Accepted Scope Adjustments
- **Dedicated email-share integration** has been deferred to backlog.
- **Premium PDF visual polish** beyond the accepted v1.4 level has been deferred to backlog.
- **Device-level notification QA evidence** has been deferred to backlog; current closeout relies on unit/integration coverage plus accepted scope adjustment.

## Known Limitations
- Report sharing currently uses the generic system share sheet rather than a purpose-built email flow.
- PDF output is ship-ready for current scope, but not yet at the final "coach-grade presentation" polish target.
- Notification delivery has been validated in code/test flows, but not yet documented with a real-device acceptance matrix.

## Next Follow-up

- Backlog: device QA matrix for notification delivery and permission behavior.
